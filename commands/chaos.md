# Chaos

Invoke the chaos skill (skills/chaos/SKILL.md) and follow it exactly.

1. `$ARGUMENTS` names a path, URL, or project → **REMIX** that target

Any doubt about register or blast radius → present 2-3 options, one recommended, and wait.

## Hard limits (from the skill, non-negotiable)

- The work stays correct; the joke is never load-bearing
